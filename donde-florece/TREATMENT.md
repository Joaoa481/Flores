# Donde florece mi cielo — treatment

Style: Firefly Pointillism (`$LIB/styles/firefly-pointillism/STYLE.md`).
Topic: a gift for the user's wife, built from the words of their own pages in this repository
("Para ti, flor de mis mañanas", "Para mi esposa: donde florece mi cielo").
Length 45 s · 1920×1080 · 60 fps · Spanish · no narration, no subtitles: one title made of points.

## 1. Three structures

1. **The planting** (single journey): a lone light plants a seed, a yellow flower grows, a cloud covers it,
   the light turns into breeze and clears the sky; the cloud becomes stars and the meadow blooms.
   Straight from the page: *"si una nube se atreve a tapar el sol, yo me convierto en brisa para despejar tu cielo"*
   and *"hoy planto esta flor amarilla en tu cielo"*.
2. **Window of stars** (before/after): an interior, a window outline, a lamp; one by one he lights stars in
   her window until the night outside is a garden. Quieter, but mostly static and close to a slideshow.
3. **A day in a flower** (countdown of hours): morning bloom, noon, storm, night. Too many beats for 45 s.

**Chosen: 1.** It has one subject (the flower, her), one goal (to bloom), one turn (the cloud) and one
native move at the peak (the breeze that blows the cloud into stars), and every line of it is already in
the user's own letter.

## 2. Logline and arc

A single light plants a yellow flower in the dark; when a cloud dims it, the light becomes a breeze,
blows the cloud into stars and the whole night blooms.

- Setup: darkness, a guiding light, a seed, a flower grows and opens.
- Turn: a cloud rolls in, rain, the flower bows and dims; near-silence.
- Ending: the light flares into a gust, the cloud scatters into stars, the flower lifts, the meadow blooms;
  the fireflies gather into the line *"Donde tú estás, la noche aprende a florecer."*

## 3. Benchmark

- *La Luna* (Pixar short, 2011) — learn: one small lyrical gesture carried by music, locked wide shots for
  wonder, an ending that reveals the sky. Don't take: characters, the moon, the brooms, any shot.
- Seurat's pointillism as grammar only — form built from discrete warm dots; nothing copied.

## 5. Beat sheet

- 0.0 black. 0.4 the guiding light focuses from a bokeh disc to a sharp point (chime).
- 1.2–3.3 it drifts, faint stars breathe in focus far away.
- 3.3–8.3 it descends in a long curve; 8.3 touches the ground (harp pluck) → ripple; grass focuses in from
  the touch point outward (8.3–10.0).
- 10.0 a stem rises from the touch point; 12.5 two leaves unfold; 14.2 a bud; 15.0–16.7 petals swirl open;
  16.7 bloom lands (harp chord + cymbal peak).
- 16.7–20.0 flower breathes and sways; the light circles it once.
- 20.0 cloud bokeh drifts in from the right; 22.5 rain streaks; 23–26 flower bows, dims to ~35 %;
  25.5–26.7 near-silence, rain only.
- 26.7 the light flares (brightest point of the film); 27.0 gust from left to right: cloud points streak,
  scatter and land in the sky as stars (28.5); rain points become fireflies.
- 30.0 flower lifts back, brighter; 31.6 synchronous flash rippling from the flower; 32–36 pull back:
  small flowers bloom across the hill.
- 36.7 fireflies swirl and gather into the title, left-aligned in the upper sky; 38.5 title lands (flash,
  glass-harp chord); held still until 44.0 (5.5 s, 44 characters → needs ≥ 4.4 s).
- 44.0–45.0 title dims, final harp note rings out.

## 7. Sound design

| Section | Ambience | Foley | Music state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | crickets, very low | chime as the light focuses | sparse single notes |
| Descent | crickets | soft shimmer on the touch | arpeggio + pad |
| Growth | crickets | rising shimmer, leaf flicks (soft) | rising arpeggios, swell |
| Breath | crickets | — | melody |
| Cloud | wind rising, rain | rain bed | minor, then near-silence |
| Gust | wind gust (whoosh) | streak whooshes | glissando, falling run |
| Bloom | crickets back | flash sweep | full |
| Title | crickets | glass chime on landing | chords, fade |

Two near-silences: 0.0–0.4 (before the first light) and 25.5–26.7 (before the flare).
Sound transitions: rain enters before the cloud is fully in (J-cut, 21.5); crickets return under the end of
the gust (L-cut, 29.5).

## 8. Title design

Spanish, two lines, left-aligned in the upper-left sky, never over the flower:
`Donde tú estás,` / `la noche aprende a florecer.` Rendered to a mask in a soft serif and sampled on a
jittered grid, one particle per sample (~2,500 points), title colour (1.0, .90, .58); held absolutely
still after landing with sub-pixel shimmer and a short flash on landing. No burned-in subtitles (no voice).

Colour ownership: the flower is the protagonist colour (1.0, .74, .34) — warm yellow, like the yellow flower
of her page; the guiding light (him) is the companion colour (.78, 1.0, .46). Neither is ever swapped.
